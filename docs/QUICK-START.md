# Fieldwork — quick start

## Open the planner

Open the `Fieldwork-Windows/Fieldwork` folder and double-click **Fieldwork.exe**. Keep the whole application folder together. The planner opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765/.

## Start an empty design

1. Click **New blank project** at the top left.
2. Enter a project name and click **Start blank project**.
3. The canvas starts with no room or walls. Click **Draw my room**, then click each corner of your outline. Click the first corner again, press **Enter**, or choose **Finish polygon** to close the room.
4. **Rectangle** and **L-shaped room** are optional starting shapes. After creating a room, set exact wall lengths and ceiling height under **Room & references**, then add doors, obstructions and equipment.

Your previous complete design is backed up before the new project starts. Use **Scenarios → Restore previous project** to bring it back. This stores the project before your most recent new-project action in this browser. Use **Export → Project & scenarios** to keep a separate project file.

## Drag and edit

| To change… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Room wall | Drag its thick edge. Its attached equipment and doors follow. |
| Room corner | Drag its square corner handle. Adjoining square walls stay square. |
| Exact wall length | Click the dimension beside that wall, enter feet and inches, then click **Apply**. |
| A wall jog | Click a wall, choose **Split wall to make a jog**, then drag either half. |
| Equipment position | Drag the equipment body. |
| Equipment size | Select one unlocked item, then drag a square resize handle. The opposite corner stays fixed. Release assemblies before resizing their members. |
| Equipment orientation | Click its on-plan **↻** control or press **R**. |
| Equipment gap | Click the green gap label and enter the required distance. This moves the item without resizing it. |
| Door position | Drag the door along a wall or onto another wall. Its opening stays on a wall that can fit it. |
| Column/obstruction | Drag its hatched body. Use **Properties** for exact dimensions and elevations. |
| Several items | Shift-click each item, or drag a box around them in empty space. |

Start dragging, then hold **Shift** to lock movement to one axis. Hold **Alt** for a fine 1/16 in. grid. **Escape** cancels a drag. **Ctrl+Z** undoes a completed change; **Ctrl+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y** redoes it.

The view stays steady during a wall drag. Crossed or collapsed walls are rejected. Unlock items or assemblies attached to a wall before moving it.

## Navigate and draw

Scroll to zoom. Hold **Space** and drag, use the middle mouse button, or choose the hand tool to pan. Press **F** to fit the room.

For a custom room, choose **Room & references → Draw room outline**. Click the corners in order, then choose **Finish polygon** or press **Enter**. **Backspace** removes the last corner; **Escape** cancels the outline.

Type lengths such as `20 ft`, `3' 6 1/2"`, or `42.5 in`. Bare numbers mean inches. All displayed dimensions and areas use imperial units.
